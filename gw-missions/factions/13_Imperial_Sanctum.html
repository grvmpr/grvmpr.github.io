Summary (click to expand)

Entering the mission has no requirements, but the only way to access the outpost is by completing Raisu Palace .

Region: Kaineng City · Mission: 12 of 13 · Type: Cooperative

Full wiki article ↗

✓ Objectives

  • Destroy Shiro [sic]

→ Walkthrough

See also: Imperial Sanctum/Guide for a detailed walkthrough

Upon completion of this mission, the players will end up in the Divine Path.

If this is the first time your character has completed this mission, remember to talk to Suun in Divine Path. There will not be any quest nor reward indicator over his head. Suun will give you the Amulet of the Mists. You can then go to one of the nearby adepts and trade the amulet for a unique item. You can only receive this amulet once per character. If for some reason you forget to talk to Suun and leave the Divine Path, you can perform the mission again and talk to Suun to receive the amulet.

✦ Tips & Notes

Skill recommendations

  • Banished Paragons or Dervishes are replaced by Bound Warriors.
  • You gain a morale boost each time a bound foe is killed.
  • The mission will succeed even if Mhenlo dies.
  • Some elementalists run this mission for money, echo chaining Celestial Storm to quickly solo Shiro and getting Master's reward.
  • The area to which Shiro banishes players contributes approximately 0.2% towards the Canthan Grandmaster Cartographer title.
    • You need to team up with at least one other player to uncover this area, as Shiro doesn't banish you if you're the only player.
    • The Spirits of the Mists in that area do not respawn after being killed, so spirit-killing skills such as Gaze of Fury help considerably if more than one banishment is planned for zone scraping at leisure.
  • Completion of this mission will fill in page #11 of the Shiro's Return storybook.
Bug. Sometimes there is a malfunction with Shiro's skill: Meditation of the Reaper. He will be able to take damage while in meditation, but the skill won't register any damage taken. It will therefore last the maximum of 30sec.
Bug. Sometimes there is a malfunction with Shiro's skill: Echoing Banishment. Heroes sometimes get taken with you when Shiro uses Echoing Banishment.
