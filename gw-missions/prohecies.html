Storyline notes

  • Pre-Searing primary quests lead into Mission 1 after the Searing.
  • Crystal Desert Ascension missions (15–17) can be done in any order.
  • Thunderhead Keep through Hell’s Precipice cannot be skipped to finish the game.
  • Foreign characters join at Lion’s Arch with Report to the White Mantle.
