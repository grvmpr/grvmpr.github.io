Storyline notes

  • Istan tutorial (or skip) leads into Chahbek Village; required heroes are noted per mission.
  • Non-Nightfall characters join at Kamadan with Battle Preparations (before Consulate Docks / The Time is Nigh).
  • Forks: Margrid (Pogahn / Dasha) vs Master of Whispers (Rilohn / Dzagonur) are mutually exclusive for heroes at that time.
  • Jennur’s Horde vs Nundu Bay are not mutually exclusive — only one is required to continue.
  • Challenge missions and The Ebony Citadel of Mallyx are optional and not listed here.
