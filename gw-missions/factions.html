Storyline notes

  • Shing Jea profession quests and secondary training lead into Minister Cho’s Estate.
  • Non-Factions characters join at Bejunkan Pier with Welcome to Cantha (before Vizunah Square).
  • After Sunjiang District the story forks Kurzick (Eternal Grove) vs Luxon (Gyala Hatchery) — both paths can be completed; only one is required.
  • Both paths rejoin at Unwaking Waters, then Taking Back the Palace → Raisu Palace → Imperial Sanctum.
  • Challenge, competitive, and elite missions (e.g. Urgoz’s Warren, The Deep) are optional and not listed here.
